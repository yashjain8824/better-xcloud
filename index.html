Sideloading & Installation Guides

Step-by-step setup guides for each platform.

Sideloading Better xCloud IPA on iOS

Choose whichever sideloading method fits your setup:

  1. Download the IPA: Download BetterXCloud_iOS.ipa from the Downloads section above to your computer or iPhone.
  2. Method 1 — Sideloadly (PC or Mac): Open Sideloadly, plug in your iPhone/iPad via USB or Wi-Fi, drag BetterXCloud_iOS.ipa into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID, and click Start.
  3. Method 2 — AltStore / SideStore: Transfer the IPA into the Files app, open AltStore or SideStore, navigate to My Apps → tap the + icon → select BetterXCloud_iOS.ipa.
  4. Method 3 — TrollStore: If running TrollStore (iOS 14.0–16.6.1), simply share or open BetterXCloud_iOS.ipa directly in TrollStore for permanent, hassle-free installation.
  5. Trust Certificate: On your iOS device, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, find your Apple ID under Developer App, and tap Trust.

Installing Better xCloud APK on Android

  1. Download APK: Tap the Download APK button above to download BetterXCloud_v0.24.2_Fixed.apk.
  2. Allow Unknown Sources: When prompted by your browser (Chrome/Firefox), tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source.
  3. Install & Launch: Tap Install. Once installed, open Better xCloud from your app drawer.
  4. Sign In & Play: Log in with your Xbox Game Pass Ultimate account. Stream settings and touch controller configurations are pre-tuned for maximum performance.

Installing via Tampermonkey / Violentmonkey

  1. Install Userscript Manager: Install the Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey extension for your browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Kiwi, or Firefox).
  2. Install Better xCloud Script: Click Install Userscript above (or open better-xcloud-optimized.user.js). Tampermonkey will prompt you to confirm the installation—click Install.
  3. Navigate to Xbox Cloud Gaming: Go to xbox.com/play.
  4. Enjoy Enhanced Stream: The Better xCloud UI button appears at the top left, giving you instant access to 1440p bitrate controls, audio boost, and HUD stats.

Running the Windows Dedicated Client

  1. Zero-Copy GPU Stream: The Windows client runs with hardware-accelerated WebRTC decoding and unthrottled background gaming.
  2. One-Click Launcher: In your Better xCloud Windows directory, launch Launch_Better_xCloud.bat or execute npm start.
  3. Full Controller Rumble: Gamepad vibration actuators are enabled natively without extra drivers or software bridges.
